# Batting

This document describes batting terms and actions used in the Cricket corpus.

## Batting stance and stroke

The batter stands in front of the wicket and uses the bat to defend the wicket or score runs. A defensive stroke is played to protect the wicket, while an attacking stroke is played to hit the ball into space.

## Runs between the wickets

A run is completed when the two batters cross and make their grounds at the opposite ends. Batters may attempt more than one run when the ball is in play. The completed runs are added to the team's score.

## Boundaries

A boundary four is scored when the ball reaches the boundary after touching the ground. A boundary six is scored when the ball clears the boundary without touching the ground. These boundary values are the scoring rules used in this corpus.

## Strike

The striker is the batter facing the delivery. The non-striker stands at the opposite end. The striker changes after a completed run, and the end also changes when an over is completed.
