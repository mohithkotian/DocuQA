# Bowling

This document describes bowling actions and over rules used in the Cricket corpus.

## The bowler

The bowler delivers the ball from one end of the pitch toward the batter at the other end. The bowler must release the ball from within the permitted area and must not unfairly alter the ball.

## Overs

An over is a sequence of six legal deliveries by one bowler from one end. After the over, a different bowler bowls the next over from the opposite end. A bowler cannot bowl two consecutive overs in the same innings.

## Delivery types

A fast delivery reaches the batter quickly, while a spin delivery turns after pitching. A yorker pitches close to the batter's feet. A bouncer is a short-pitched delivery that rises toward the batter. These descriptions are general labels, not separate scoring events.

## Illegal deliveries

A no-ball and a wide are illegal deliveries. Each gives the batting side an extra and requires another delivery before the over can contain six legal deliveries.
