# Fielding

This document describes fielding positions and dismissals used in the Cricket corpus.

## Fielding positions

The wicketkeeper fields directly behind the striker's wicket. Slip fielders stand beside the wicketkeeper on the off side. A cover fielder protects a region on the off side in front of square. A fine leg fielder is positioned on the leg side behind square.

## Catches

A catch dismisses the batter when a fielder completes control of the ball before it touches the ground. The catch must be completed within the field of play under the rules used by this corpus.

## Run outs and stumpings

A run out occurs when a batter is out of their ground and a fielder breaks the relevant wicket with the ball. A stumping occurs when the wicketkeeper breaks the wicket while the batter is out of the ground and is not attempting a run.

## Fielding objective

The fielding side tries to prevent runs and dismiss the batters. Fielders return the ball to the wicketkeeper or bowler to control the batters' movement between the wickets.
