# Scoring

This document defines scoring terms used in the Cricket corpus.

## Team total

A team's score is the total of runs scored by batters plus extras. The score is commonly written as runs for wickets, such as 150 for 4, meaning 150 runs have been scored and four wickets have fallen.

## Batting runs

Batters score runs by running between the wickets or by hitting boundaries. A boundary four is worth four runs when the ball reaches the boundary after touching the ground. A boundary six is worth six runs when the ball clears the boundary without touching the ground.

## Extras

Extras are runs not credited to the batter. The extras in this corpus are no-balls, wides, byes, and leg byes. A no-ball gives one no-ball extra and a wide gives one wide extra unless additional runs are completed or awarded.

## Byes and leg byes

A bye is an extra run taken when the ball passes the batter without touching the bat or body. A leg bye is an extra run taken after the ball touches the batter's body when the batter has tried to play the ball or avoid it.
