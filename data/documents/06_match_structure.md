# Match Structure

This document describes match formats and innings structure used in the Cricket corpus.

## Innings

An innings is a team's opportunity to bat and score runs. In a limited-overs match, each team has a set number of overs. The innings ends when the allotted overs are complete or the side loses all of its wickets, subject to the competition rules.

## Test match

A Test match is scheduled for up to five days. Each team normally has two innings. The result can be a win, a loss, a draw, or a tie.

## One-day match

A one-day international match gives each team one innings with a limit of 50 overs. The team with the higher score after both innings wins, unless the match has a tie or another result under its playing conditions.

## Twenty20 match

A Twenty20 match gives each team one innings with a limit of 20 overs. The short format uses the same basic concepts of deliveries, wickets, runs, and extras.
