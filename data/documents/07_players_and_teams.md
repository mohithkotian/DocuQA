# Players and Teams

This document defines team and player roles used in the Cricket corpus.

## Team size

A cricket team has eleven players. During an innings, the batting side has two batters on the field at a time, while the other team places its bowler and fielders around the ground.

## Captain and wicketkeeper

The captain leads the team and chooses fielding positions and bowling changes. The wicketkeeper fields behind the striker's wicket and may take catches or make stumpings.

## Batters and bowlers

A batter's primary role is to score runs and protect the wicket. A bowler's primary role is to deliver legal balls, prevent runs, and take wickets. An all-rounder contributes substantially as both a batter and a bowler.

## Substitutes

A substitute fielder may field for an absent player under the applicable playing conditions, but the substitute does not automatically take the absent player's batting or bowling role.
