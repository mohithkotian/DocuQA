# Tournaments

This document describes tournament structures used in the Cricket corpus.

## League stage

A league stage is a round-robin phase in which teams play scheduled matches and receive competition points according to the tournament rules. Teams are ranked on the points table.

## Knockout stage

A knockout stage eliminates a team after a match or series loss. A semi-final is a knockout match that determines a finalist. The final determines the tournament champion.

## Tournament formats

A tournament may use Test, one-day, or Twenty20 matches. The tournament format determines the match length and innings limits, while the competition rules define points, qualification, and tie-break procedures.

## Competition records

The corpus does not define the winner, host country, dates, or attendance of any named tournament. Questions asking for those facts are deliberately unanswerable from this document collection.
