# Cricket Basics

A cricket match is played between two teams of eleven players. The batting side scores runs while the bowling and fielding side tries to dismiss batters.

## Overs and innings

An over consists of six legal deliveries. A bowler changes ends after each over. In a one-day match, each team normally receives a limited number of overs, while a Test match can continue for several days.

## Fielding positions

The wicketkeeper stands behind the stumps. Fielders may be placed in the slips, at cover, or near the boundary. A catch taken before the ball touches the ground dismisses the batter.
